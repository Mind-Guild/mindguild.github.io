The paper

A five-layer architecture for S3Q theory

We propose a five-layer implementation architecture for the S3Q (Simulated, Situated, Structurally Coherent) theory of consciousness. Rather than introducing novel formalisms, the architecture composes published computational primitives into a single pipeline, mapping each tenet to compatible machinery with defined interfaces, a developmental bootstrap sequence, and falsifiable predictions the conjunction produces that no subset does in isolation.

Background

What does S3Q mean?

S3Q stands for Simulated, Situated, Structurally Coherent Qualia. The theory names three jointly necessary conditions for qualia, defining what consciousness requires at the functional level. Our paper turns each one into machinery you can build and test.

Situated

Grounded sensorimotor situatedness: the system acts in a world and senses the results of its own actions.

Simulated

Internal simulation through a world model: the system can imagine what would happen before it acts.

Structurally coherent

Structural coherence between predictions and observations: what the system expects and what it senses have to fit together.
