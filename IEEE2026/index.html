The paper

A five-layer architecture for S3Q theory

We propose a five-layer implementation architecture for the S3Q (Simulated, Situated, Structurally Coherent) theory of consciousness. Rather than introducing novel formalisms, the architecture composes published computational primitives into a single pipeline, mapping each tenet to compatible machinery with defined interfaces, a developmental bootstrap sequence, and falsifiable predictions the conjunction produces that no subset does in isolation.
