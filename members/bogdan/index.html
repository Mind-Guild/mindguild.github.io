Bogdan Udrea

Aerospace engineer · Spacecraft autonomy

Bogdan Udrea is an aerospace engineer and founder of VisSidus Technologies. His research spans spacecraft dynamics and control, formation flying, mission design, and onboard autonomy. He earned his Ph.D. in aeronautics and astronautics at the University of Washington. His work on cooperative satellite missions connects distributed control with the challenges of operating in complex physical environments.

Research focus
  • Spacecraft autonomy
  • Distributed control
  • Satellite swarms
