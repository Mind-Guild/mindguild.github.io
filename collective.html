Luis El Srouji

Neuromorphic computing researcher · Photonic neural networks

Luis El Srouji researches brain-inspired computing at the University of California, Davis. His work explores photonic and optoelectronic neural networks, biologically motivated learning rules, and computing architectures that reflect the diversity of neural dynamics. He also writes for Mind Guild about how primary and learned value can inform adaptable artificial intelligence.

Research focus
  • Neuromorphic computing
  • Photonic neural networks
